# summa-ai
A collaborative AI council where multiple LLMs discuss and debate your query in a group chat to deliver a superior output. It's brainstorming, supercharged. A think tank of LLMs.

## Description
SUMMA-LLMs is a debate platform that allows users to engage in structured discussions with AI assistants. Users can create conversations where multiple AI models present different perspectives on topics, with judge AI models evaluating the quality of arguments. The platform supports various leading AI providers and includes features for managing conversation history, customizing model preferences, and real-time collaboration. It's designed for users who want to explore complex topics through AI-mediated debates and get comprehensive viewpoints on important decisions or ideas.
